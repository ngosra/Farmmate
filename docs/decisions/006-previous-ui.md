# Previous FarmMate screens

The September 2025 screens live in the shared Drive folder `farmmate`. They were inspected on 25 November 2026. They were not in the repository before that.

The visual language is a bright green sidebar (`#0AB187`), white content on a light gray page, a split login with a white line drawing of a farmer, and rounded white cards. The sidebar says hello by name and groups work under Dashboard, Reports, Farm Activities, Settings, Notifications, and Sign out. Farm Activities opens farmers, land parcels, inspections, inputs, production, traceability, training, and certification. The assistant is a chat, with the assistant bubble in green and the person's bubble in gray.

The old dashboard also shows weather, pest level, profit margin, an aerial photograph, and an NDVI curve. Those values in the screenshots are sample art. The app shows those slots only when a record exists. Weather and pest stay empty until a feed is connected. NDVI stays empty until an observation is stored. A readiness percent is still not a certificate.
